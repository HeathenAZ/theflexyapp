Privacy Policy

Flexy Block Calculator · Effective November 15, 2026

Flexy Block Calculator (“Flexy”, “the app”) helps delivery drivers estimate what a delivery block pays after vehicle costs and taxes. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your phone. Flexy has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. The app does not collect, send, sell or share your personal information.

Information you enter

You may enter vehicle details (such as fuel price and miles per gallon), block pay, miles driven, hours worked, a station code, a target hourly rate and tax preferences. This information is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device and is used only to calculate your results inside the app.

Calendar access

If you allow it, Flexy reads events in the calendars on your phone (including a Google Calendar synced to the phone) whose title or notes contain the keyword you choose, such as “Amazon Flex”. From those events it uses only the date, start and end time, title, notes and location to work out block pay and length. Calendar data is read on your device, is never sent anywhere, and the app never changes or deletes your calendar events. You can turn calendar reading off in the app or remove the permission in Android Settings at any time.

Files you choose

If you import a mileage file (CSV) or restore a backup, the app reads only the file you pick, on your device. Backups you create are saved where you choose (such as your Downloads folder) and are under your control.

Android backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include Flexy’s saved log in your device backup (for example, your Google account backup) so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Android and your backup provider under their privacy terms; the developer of Flexy cannot access it.

Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive any information from the app. The app does not use the internet.

Children

Flexy is intended for adult delivery drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

Not affiliated with Amazon

Flexy is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Amazon. Amazon Flex is a trademark of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates. Tax figures in the app are rough estimates, not tax advice.
