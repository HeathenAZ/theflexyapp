Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email drchapman42@gmail.com and include your phone model and Android version if something isn’t working.

My blocks aren’t showing up from the calendar

  • Make sure the calendar permission is on: Android Settings → Apps → Flexy Block Calculator → Permissions → Calendar.
  • Your calendar events need to contain your keyword (More → Google Calendar → “Calendar events contain”, default “Amazon Flex”) and the pay, like “$87.50”.
  • Only finished blocks are added automatically. Upcoming blocks appear on the Today screen.
  • If you use Google Calendar, check that the account is syncing in your phone’s calendar app.

Why does a day say “needs miles”?

Flexy needs the miles you drove to work out gas and wear. Type them on the Today screen, or import a trip export (CSV) from your mileage tracker under More → Mileage import.

How are the numbers worked out?

Net = pay − (miles × your cost per mile) − tolls. Cost per mile comes from your fuel price and MPG (or electricity cost and miles per kWh) plus a wear allowance. Tax set-aside is a rough US estimate using self-employment tax and your income-tax rate after the IRS standard mileage deduction. It isn’t tax advice.

Moving to a new phone

Use More → Backup & restore → Save backup, copy the file to the new phone, then Restore a backup. If Android backup is on, your log is also included in your phone’s Google backup.

Deleting your data

Flexy has no account and stores nothing off your phone. Delete individual days in the Log, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
